B1 Level Lesson

Life in a New City

Mastering used to, be used to, and get used to

1

Meet Carlos

Carlos lived in a small, quiet town in Venezuela for 25 years. He woke up late, walked everywhere, and ate homemade food every day. Last month, he moved to a busy, noisy capital city for a new job. Everything is different for him now!

🏠 His Past Life

  • • Woke up late
  • • Walked everywhere
  • • Quiet environment

🏙️ His Present Life

  • • Heavy traffic & noise
  • • Fast-paced routine
  • • Adapting day by day
2

Choose the Correct Structure

Based on Carlos's story, select the right option for each sentence.

1. Carlos ______ up late when he lived in his small town.

2. Now, Carlos lives in a loud city with heavy traffic, but after a month, he ______ the noise.

3. In the beginning, traffic was terrible, but slowly he ______ driving in the busy streets.

3

Side-by-Side Comparison

Notice how a small change in words completely changes the meaning.

Pair A: History vs. Comfort

“I used to work night shifts.”

👉 Meaning: I did this in the past, but I don't anymore.

“I am used to working night shifts.”

👉 Meaning: It is normal and familiar for me right now.

Pair B: Process vs. Past Dislike

“She got used to the cold weather.”

👉 Meaning: The process of adaptation happened successfully.

“She didn't use to like the cold weather.”

👉 Meaning: In the past, her opinion or habit was different.

4

Speaking & Fashion Connection

Q1

What is something you used to wear when you were younger that you don't wear anymore?

Q2
